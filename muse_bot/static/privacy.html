Muse Hospitality Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Muse Hospitality ("Muse", "we") runs nightlife guest lists in New York City. This policy explains what we collect when you message our Instagram account, @musehospitalitynyc, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram username, display name and Instagram user ID.
  • The messages you send us and our replies.
  • Details of your request: the night and venue, how many people are in your group, how many are guys, and whether everyone is over 21.
  • Whether you arrived at the venue and how many people in your group checked in.
  • If you mention who referred you, that person's name.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages, put your group on the guest list and send you venue details.
  • To let our hosts know who to expect at the door.
  • To send you a thank-you message, or a note if we missed you, after the night.
  • To credit the person who referred you.
  • To see how quickly we reply and how our nights go, so we can improve.

Some replies are sent automatically by our messaging assistant. A member of our team can always step in.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share only what is needed to get you in: your name and group size go to the host on duty, and to the venue when the venue asks for a guest list. Our service providers (Meta, which runs Instagram messaging, and our hosting providers) process it for us under their own terms.

Messages we send

We only message you after you message us, and only within the time Instagram allows. We never send cold messages.

How long we keep it

We keep guest list records for up to 2 years, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your choices and data deletion

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, send a DM to @musehospitalitynyc saying "delete my data". We will delete your messages and guest list records within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Age

Our events are for guests 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If we learn we have, we delete it.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions? DM us at @musehospitalitynyc.
